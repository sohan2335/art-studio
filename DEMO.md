# Demo walkthrough

A silent, live-browser walkthrough has been prepared in the local workspace's `outputs/demo/` folder:

- `Art_Studio_Walkthrough.mp4`: 4 minutes 20.73 seconds, H.264 MP4.
- `Art_Studio_Narration.txt`: a timed script for the candidate's own narration.
- `README.txt`: recording details and remaining steps.

The footage shows signup, painting purchase, wall booking with calculated price, pottery request, studio login and approvals, followed by a read-only view of the actual purchase handler and transaction helper in `server.js`. It uses actual browser interactions rather than presentation slides. Browser frames are sampled at approximately three frames per second and exported at 30 fps. Gaps between chapter captures are omitted.

An isolated in-memory database and artificial demo accounts keep the owner's real records untouched. The recording does not add a demo endpoint or accounts to the production project. The source-code view exists only in the temporary recording server.

The MP4 is intentionally silent. The candidate must add their own voice, review the complete export and keep it under five minutes before submission. No upload, sharing change or assignment submission has been performed.
